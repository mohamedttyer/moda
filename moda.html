Chip

The Apple M2 chip represents a massive leap forward in custom silicon performance and energy efficiency, built on an enhanced 5-nanometer process. Featuring an 8-core CPU and up to a 10-core GPU, it delivers faster processing speeds and superior graphics performance compared to its predecessor, all while maintaining the hallmark efficiency that allows devices like the MacBook Air to run cool and quiet without a fan. Additionally, the M2 incorporates a next-generation Neural Engine capable of handling up to 15.8 trillion operations per second to accelerate machine learning tasks, alongside a unified memory architecture that provides high bandwidth and low latency for seamless multitasking across demanding creative and professional applications.

Battery

The Apple M2 MacBook Air battery is designed for exceptional all-day efficiency, delivering up to 18 hours of video playback on a single charge thanks to the advanced power-saving architecture of the M2 chip. Powered by a built-in 52.6-watt-hour lithium-polymer battery, it easily gets students and professionals through a full workday of web browsing, document editing, and multitasking without needing to hunt for an outlet. Additionally, it supports fast charging with a compatible 67W or higher USB-C power adapter, allowing you to quickly juice up to 50% battery in about 30 minutes when you're on the go.
